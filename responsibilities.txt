  → coordinate detection, overlap resolution, pseudonyms, and replacement


methods for deidentifying:
Regex — emails, phone numbers, IDs, dates, account numbers, IPs, etc.
Named-entity recognition (NER) — people, organizations, locations, dates.
Dictionary matching — cities, hospitals, schools, government agencies, etc.
Contextual classification — detect sensitive information that doesn’t follow a fixed pattern.
Cross-field analysis — determine whether combinations of attributes could identify somebody.
Document metadata inspection — author names, comments, tracked changes, filenames, EXIF data, embedded document properties.
Link/identifier analysis — URLs and references that contain tokens or identifiers.
Consistency-preserving replacement — replace the same person with the same pseudonym throughout the document.
